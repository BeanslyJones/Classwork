04 · TWO TOUCHING FIELDS

One test ship, two rad fields. Press anywhere to grab the ship, drag to aim, lift to release. BOOST (or Space) = maneuvering. Your field (left) recharges you while you cruise; hard turns, boosting, or being shot suspend that. The other field bleeds you by the wheel: attuned < strong < neutral < weak. The seam is battery-weighted — drain a battery and watch it walk.
