01 · THE JUGGLE

Mouse or touch: press near a ship to grab it. Drag to aim (heading is from the ship, not your finger). Lift to fling — full momentum restored.

Keys 1–4: grab that ship directly. R: restart. N: new seed. When all ships are gone, tap anywhere to restart.

Drain is the clock. Stopped ships crystallize. Crystals kill on contact. Dead ships become crystals.
