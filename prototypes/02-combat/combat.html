02 · COMBAT

Blue = yours, red = opposition (same class table). Only capitals (big ships) have turrets; every other ship's guns fire straight ahead, so aim by pointing the ship. Press near a blue ship to grab, drag to aim, lift to fling. DODGE (or Space) barrel-rolls the held ship: straight line, big energy cost, turn lockout after.

Guns are automatic and lock the first enemy to enter the arc. Where it entered sets the lock's ceiling: dead centre can reach a full lock, the edge stays weak. Holding it in the arc builds the lock, which tightens spread, leads the target and speeds up reloads. Lock line: green = centre entry, red = edge; brighter = stronger lock. Torpedo bombers (6) fire slow torpedoes that do 3× to capitals. One energy pool = health + ammo + boost. F: refocus. 1–6: grab bank. R: restart. N: new seed. Game speed (bottom left) slows the whole battle down; P pauses.

Camera (top right, C cycles): Fit keeps all your ships in frame, Snap zooms to the selected ship, Bubble keeps the wide view and magnifies around it. Grabbing a ship selects it; tap empty space to deselect (closes the bubble). The slider under the buttons is the current mode's zoom. Ships hidden under the bubble show through as outlines.

Capitals have weak points (rings on the hull): bridge (nose) kills the turret, engines (tail) cripple speed, vents (sides) bleed energy. Hits there do extra damage.
