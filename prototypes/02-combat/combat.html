AMANN ASSAULT · COMBAT

Blue = yours, red = opposition (same class table). Only capitals (big ships) have turrets; every other ship's guns fire straight ahead, so aim by pointing the ship. Press near a blue ship to grab, drag to aim, lift to fling. DODGE (or Space) barrel-rolls the held ship: straight line, big energy cost, turn lockout after.

Guns are automatic and lock the first enemy to enter the arc. Where it entered sets the lock's ceiling: dead centre can reach a full lock, the edge stays weak. Holding it in the arc builds the lock, which tightens spread, leads the target and speeds up reloads. Lock line: green = centre entry, red = edge; brighter = stronger lock. Torpedo bombers (6) fire slow torpedoes that do 3× to capitals. One energy pool = health + ammo + boost. T (or F): retarget. Click/tap a ship or press 1–9 to select it; click the map to send it that way (or drag the ship). Click the selected ship again or Esc to deselect. R: restart. N: new seed. Game speed (bottom left) slows the whole battle down; P pauses.

Camera (top right, C cycles): Fit keeps all your ships in frame, Snap zooms to the selected ship, Bubble keeps the wide view and magnifies around it. Grabbing a ship selects it; tap it again (or Esc) to deselect (closes the bubble). The slider under the buttons is the current mode's zoom. Ships hidden under the bubble show through as outlines.

Built for landscape: one thumb drags ships, the other hits DODGE and RETARGET (bottom right). The bubble magnifies the middle of the selected ship's firing arc.

The AI matches your APM (actions per minute: grabs, orders, dodges, retargets): each AI action re-aims one of its ships, so it juggles at your pace. Both show top left.

Cones are split green / yellow / red: an enemy entering through the green middle can be fully locked, the red edges barely. You always see your cones; enemy cones show only near your ships (CAMERA → enemyConePx). Brackets snap onto a ship's new target when it switches; RETARGET makes a bigger cue and a beep.

Destroy the Carrier: earn money every second and by flying over wrecks; build ships from your carrier (Q W E A S D or the bar at the bottom); kill the enemy carrier to win. One Grayzer per side.

Capitals have weak points (rings on the hull): bridge (nose) kills the turret, engines (tail) cripple speed, vents (sides) bleed energy. Hits there do extra damage.
