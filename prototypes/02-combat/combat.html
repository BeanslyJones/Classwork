02 · COMBAT

Blue = yours, red = opposition (same class table). Only capitals (big ships) have turrets; every other ship's guns fire straight ahead, so aim by pointing the ship. Press near a blue ship to grab, drag to aim, lift to fling. DODGE (or Space) barrel-rolls the held ship: straight line, big energy cost, turn lockout after.

Guns are automatic: cones acquire first entrant, cooldown only ticks on a valid target, spread tightens with lock. One energy pool = health + ammo + boost. F: refocus. 1–5: grab bank. R: restart. N: new seed. Game speed (bottom left) slows the whole battle down; P pauses.

Camera (top right, C cycles): Fit keeps all your ships in frame, Snap zooms to the ship you're flying (or last flew), Bubble keeps the wide view and magnifies around that ship.

Capitals have weak points (rings on the hull): bridge (nose) kills the turret, engines (tail) cripple speed, vents (sides) bleed energy. Hits there do extra damage.
