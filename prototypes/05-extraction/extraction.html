05 · EXTRACTION

Your dropship (big, green ring) is the mission clock — its battery runs the resupply and the climb home. The harvester mines crystals on its own; keep it alive. The diamond is the battery vessel: grab and park it inside an enemy to siphon them, ZAP to blast, DETONATE for the big goodbye (leaves rich salvage).

LAUNCH any time: heavier hold = longer, shootable climb. Run the battery dry — on the ground or mid-climb — and you strand and crystallize.

Press near a blue ship to grab · drag to aim · lift to fling · Space dodge · L launch.
